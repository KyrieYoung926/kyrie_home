Xun Kyrie Yang

Robotics · Whole-body control · Reinforcement learning

I am Xun Kyrie Yang, a visiting student in the ML & Articulated Robots Group at the Oxford Robotics Institute, supervised by Prof. Ioannis Havoutis. Feel free to get in touch about robotics and research collaborations.

I completed my M.Phil. in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) in October 2026, working in Precognition Lab under the supervision of Prof. Junwei Liang. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, where I was supervised by Prof. Yangkun Zhang.

My research focuses on humanoid robots, whole-body control, and reinforcement learning. I am interested in combining perception, motion imitation, and learned world models to enable agile physical interaction and collaborative loco-manipulation.

I am currently working on SwiftCatch, an egocentric framework for agile whole-body ball catching on humanoid robots. My previous work includes humanoid tennis, safe locomotion, autonomous loco-manipulation, and whole-body teleoperation.

Research interests

  • Perception-driven whole-body control and dynamic humanoid interaction
  • Reinforcement learning, motion imitation, and sim-to-real transfer

I also share Isaac Lab tutorials on Bilibili, with 3k subscribers and over 100k views.
