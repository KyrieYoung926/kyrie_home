## Perception-Driven Humanoid Bimanual Catching

[Open the CatchItMan project homepage ↗](https://play.kyrielab.com)

[![CatchItMan interactive humanoid ball-catching simulation]({{ '/assets/img/research/catchitman.png' | relative_url }})](https://play.kyrielab.com)

**Status: ongoing**

Developing a whole-body reinforcement learning framework for dynamic single-handed and bimanual ball catching on humanoid robots. The framework integrates depth-based perception / MoCap, ball trajectory prediction, policy distillation, and sim-to-real transfer.

[Try the interactive demo](https://play.kyrielab.com)
