## SwiftCatch: Egocentric Agile Whole-Body Ball Catching for Humanoid Robots

[Open the SwiftCatch project homepage ↗](https://play.kyrielab.com)

[![SwiftCatch interactive humanoid ball-catching simulation]({{ '/assets/img/research/swiftcatch.png' | relative_url }})](https://play.kyrielab.com)

**Status: ongoing**

Developing an egocentric whole-body reinforcement learning framework for dynamic single-handed and bimanual ball catching on humanoid robots. The framework integrates depth-based perception / MoCap, ball trajectory prediction, policy distillation, and sim-to-real transfer.

[Try the interactive demo](https://play.kyrielab.com)
